About me

Expertise, trust and exclusivity — in every negotiation.

A business administrator by training and a specialist in Real Estate Business and Developments, I built more than 12 years of solid career in corporate management at multinational companies, with stints at Caixa Econômica Federal and in construction in João Pessoa, before returning to the real estate market. Today I work as a specialist broker associated with Invista Imóveis, in the new-launches team, and as a real estate expert and appraiser (CNAI).

Read my full story

At multinationals, I worked in sustainability, land management and regularization, asset management, standards certifications and management systems. My path, however, began even earlier. At Caixa Econômica Federal, I got close to housing finance and banking relationships. In construction in João Pessoa, in commercial management, I worked directly with sales, after-sales, notary offices and contracts, and set up the credit recovery department. This hands-on market experience — from someone who has been on both sides of the counter — is a background I carry with me to this day.

My education is in Business Administration (Faculdade Internacional da Paraíba) and includes a Specialization in Real Estate Developments and Business (Unicorp Faculdades), an MBA in Strategic People Management (Unipê) and postgraduate degrees in Integrated Management Systems — ISO Standards (Unyleya) and in Environmental Control Engineering (PUC Minas).

This is the background I bring to every client: process discipline, data reading and a long-term vision — not just brokering a deal, but real consulting. As a real estate expert and appraiser, I have courses in Market Appraisal and Expert Reports and in Judicial Expert Reports, and I am a Delegate of CRECI-PB (the Paraíba real estate regulatory council) — 21st Region.
